Why He Came Back

The Question That Started This

This semester changed me in ways I didn't see coming. I started it carrying a version of myself I wasn't sure I liked, uncertain, stretched thin, wondering if I was really supposed to be here. And somewhere in the middle of studying these two ministries, something shifted. When Christ appeared to the Nephites, He had already completed the Atonement. He had already been resurrected. He didn't have to come back. That's what made me stop and actually look at what He chose to do when He did, what He chose to say again, and what He chose to say differently. Because He came back on purpose.

Scripture Pairings

The Parallels

Pairing 01 of 07

The Beatitudes

New Testament Matthew 5:3–5

“Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted. Blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth.”

Parallel
Book of Mormon 3 Nephi 12:3–5

“Yea, blessed are the poor in spirit who come unto me, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. And again, blessed are all they that mourn, for they shall be comforted. And blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth.”

He says it again, with one addition: who come unto me. In the Nephite record, the blessing is tied not just to a condition of heart, but to turning toward Him.

Pairing 02 of 07

The Sacrament

New Testament Luke 22:19–20

“And he took bread, and gave thanks, and brake it, and gave unto them, saying, This is my body which is given for you: this do in remembrance of me. Likewise also the cup after supper, saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood.”

Parallel
Book of Mormon 3 Nephi 18:7, 11

“And this shall ye always observe to do, even as I have done… And this shall ye do in remembrance of my blood, which I have shed for you, that ye may witness unto the Father that ye do always remember me.”

After the Atonement is complete, He administers it again. This time the past tense is different. He doesn’t say will be shed. He says have shed. It is finished, and it is still personal.

Pairing 03 of 07

The Lord’s Prayer

New Testament Matthew 6:9–10

“After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven.”

Parallel
Book of Mormon 3 Nephi 13:9–10

“After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name. Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven.”

The prayer appears again almost word for word. What struck me about this: He chose to repeat it. Of everything He could have taught, this made the list twice. Prayer was worth saying again.

Pairing 04 of 07

Healing the Afflicted

New Testament Matthew 14:14

“And Jesus went forth, and saw a great multitude, and was moved with compassion toward them, and he healed their sick.”

Parallel
Book of Mormon 3 Nephi 17:6–7

“And he said unto them: Behold, my bowels are filled with compassion towards you. Have ye any that are sick among you?… bring them hither and I will heal them.”

In the Nephite account, He says the feeling out loud: my bowels are filled with compassion. He had just said He needed to go. Then He turned around. He stayed because He felt what they felt, and that mattered more than the schedule.

Pairing 05 of 07

Come Unto Me

New Testament Matthew 11:28–29

“Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls.”

Parallel
Book of Mormon 3 Nephi 9:14

“Yea, verily I say unto you, if ye will come unto me ye shall have eternal life. Behold, mine arm of mercy is extended towards you, and whosoever will come, him will I receive.”

Now the invitation comes from the other side. He has already done it. The cross is past. The descent into suffering is past. And He is still saying: Come. That’s the part that stays with me.

Pairing 06 of 07

The Children

New Testament Matthew 19:14

“But Jesus said, Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.”

Parallel
Book of Mormon 3 Nephi 17:11–12

“And it came to pass that he commanded that their little children should be brought. So they brought their little children and set them down upon the ground round about him, and Jesus stood in the midst; and the multitude gave way till they had all been brought unto him.”

In Matthew it is one sentence. In the Nephite record, He weeps, He prays, angels surround the children in fire. The New Testament tells you the principle. The Book of Mormon shows you what it actually looked like when He meant it. The expansion is the point.

Pairing 07 of 07

Faith and Doubt

New Testament Matthew 14:28–31

“And Peter answered him and said, Lord, if it be thou, bid me come unto thee on the water. And he said, Come. And when Peter was come down out of the ship, he walked on the water, to go to Jesus. But when he saw the wind boisterous, he was afraid; and beginning to sink, he cried, saying, Lord, save me. And immediately Jesus stretched forth his hand, and caught him, and said unto him, O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?”

Parallel
Book of Mormon 3 Nephi 19:35

“And it came to pass that when Jesus had made an end of praying he came again to the disciples, and said unto them: So great faith have I never seen among all the Jews; wherefore I could not show unto them so great miracles, because of their unbelief.”

In Israel He marvels at the doubt. Among the Nephites He marvels at the faith. The same Christ, two different responses, separated by a resurrection and an ocean. It makes you wonder what He sees when He looks at you.

Personal Witness

What He Has Done for Me

I grew up in Queens, New York. First person in my family to go to college. There were plenty of moments where I genuinely did not know if I was supposed to be here, in accounting, at BYU, in any of it. The gap between where I started and where I was trying to go felt enormous some nights.

I believe in the Atonement of Jesus Christ. Not just as doctrine. I believe it the way you believe something after you have actually needed it and felt it work. His grace covered the things I have done wrong and it covered the version of me that did not think he deserved to be here. Both of those things needed covering.

He descended below all things so He would know exactly where you are when you are at your lowest. I have been in places where I felt completely alone, and I felt something else there too. That something else is what I am talking about. His grace does not wait until you are good enough. It meets you in the gap. It met me in mine.

That is what this whole project kept pointing me back to. He said come unto me in Israel. He said it again among the Nephites. He keeps saying it because He means it for every person who thinks they are too far gone or too small or too late. He meant it for me this year.

I landed internships at Deloitte and Grant Thornton, firms I would have been afraid to apply to a couple years ago. I got into graduate school. I am married to someone who makes me want to be better. None of that happened because I had it figured out. It happened because I kept moving and He kept showing up. I did not get here alone. I am not the same person I was at the start of this semester, and that is entirely because of Him.

This video says it better than I can.
